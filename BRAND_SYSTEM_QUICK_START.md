# Seller Brand System - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 For Sellers

### Creating Your Brand

1. **Login as Seller**
   - Navigate to Seller Dashboard (`/seller/dashboard`)

2. **Access Brand Setup**
   - Click "Brend sozlamalari" button in the header
   - Or go directly to `/brand-setup`

3. **Fill Brand Information**
   - **Brend nomi** (required): Your brand name
   - **Brend logotipi** (optional): URL to your logo image
   - **Brend tavsifi** (optional): Short description of your brand

4. **Save**
   - Click "Yaratish" to create or "Yangilash" to update
   - Your brand will appear on all your products

### Brand Benefits

✅ **Professional Display**
- Your brand logo appears on product cards
- Brand name is prominently displayed
- "Sotuvchi" badge shows you're a branded seller

✅ **Seller Profile Page**
- Customers can click your brand to see all your products
- Your brand description is displayed
- All your products are shown in one place

✅ **Better Visibility**
- Branded products stand out
- Customers can easily find all your products
- Professional appearance builds trust

## 👥 For Customers

### Finding Branded Products

1. **On Product Cards**
   - Look for brand logo and name
   - "Sotuvchi" badge indicates branded seller
   - "Oddiy sotuvchi" badge for non-branded sellers

2. **Click Brand Name**
   - Opens seller profile page
   - See all products from that seller
   - View brand description and ratings

3. **Product Detail Page**
   - Brand info at the top
   - Click to visit seller profile
   - See related products from same brand

### Related Products

- Scroll to bottom of product detail page
- "O'xshash mahsulotlar" section shows similar items
- Based on same category or same brand

## 📸 Product Image Guidelines

### For Best Results

**Image Specifications**:
- Size: 1080 x 1080 pixels (square)
- Background: White (recommended)
- Format: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- File size: Under 5MB

**What to Show**:
- Full product visible (no cropping)
- Clear, well-lit photo
- Product centered
- Professional appearance

**What to Avoid**:
- Blurry images
- Dark backgrounds
- Cropped products
- Low resolution

---

## 🎨 How Images Are Displayed

### Product Cards
- Square format (1:1 ratio)
- White background
- No cropping (full product visible)

### Product Detail Page

- Up to 4 images per product

### Mobile vs Desktop
- **Desktop**: Image left, description right

## 🆘 Common Questions

### Q: Do I need a brand to sell?
**A**: No! You can sell without a brand. You'll be shown as "Oddiy sotuvchi" (regular seller).

### Q: Can I change my brand later?
**A**: Yes! Go to Brand Setup and update anytime.

### Q: What if I don't have a logo?
**A**: Logo is optional. Your brand name will still be displayed.

### Q: How many images can I upload per product?
**A**: Minimum 1, maximum 4 images per product.

### Q: What happens if my image isn't square?
**A**: The system will display it with white padding to maintain square format.

### Q: Can customers see all my products?
**A**: Yes! They can click your brand name to see your seller profile with all products.
